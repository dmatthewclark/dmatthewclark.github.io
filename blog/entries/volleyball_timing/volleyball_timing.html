Volleyball Timing

Introduction

Even if you have perfect footwork and hitting technique, you may not be ready to make an effective attack. This is the case for me at the moment. I have decent motions with my foot work and swinging arm, but seem to always end up with the ball out of range once I'm in the air. There are various factors to consider to identify why this is happening.

  1. Set Height
  2. Set Destination
  3. Point of attack
  4. Approach Speed
